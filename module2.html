Sensors & Actuators

Learn how IoT devices sense their environment and perform physical actions.

📡 What is a Sensor?

A sensor is an electronic device that detects physical conditions from the environment and converts them into useful data.

Sensors are very important in IoT because they allow devices to understand what is happening around them.

Examples of Sensors

  • 🌡️ Temperature Sensor – measures temperature
  • 💡 Light Sensor – detects light intensity
  • 🚶 Motion Sensor – detects movement
  • 💧 Humidity Sensor – measures humidity

⚙️ What is an Actuator?

An actuator is a device that converts an electrical control signal into a physical action.

Actuators allow an IoT system to perform an action based on the information received from sensors.

Examples of Actuators

  • 💡 LED – produces light
  • ⚙️ Motor – produces movement
  • 🔊 Buzzer – produces sound
  • 🔌 Relay – controls electrical devices

🏠 Real-World Example: Smart Home

Consider a smart home system. A motion sensor detects a person entering a room. The controller processes the information and turns on a light using an actuator.

This demonstrates how sensors and actuators work together in an IoT system.
